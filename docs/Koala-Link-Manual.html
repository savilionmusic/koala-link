Koala Link 1.4.10

Play Koala Sampler from an Akai MPC Touch or Ableton Push 2, and film your performances with the built-in Webcam.

App version 1.4.10, build 23 · Apple silicon · macOS 13 or later · Free

1. What you need

  • An Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later.
  • Koala Sampler for Mac, bought and installed separately.
  • MPC Touch: DisplayLink Manager (installed separately) for the touchscreen. The touchscreen driver is included in the installer.
  • Push 2 (preview): connected by USB. Close Ableton Live while Koala Link uses the Push.
  • Webcam: a camera, plus macOS permission for Camera and for Screen & System Audio Recording (used to record Koala's sound).

2. Install

  1. Download Koala-Link-1.4.10-Installer.pkg and open it. Follow the steps and enter your Mac password.
  2. The app is not notarized. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Open Koala Link from Applications and choose your controller. Connect and power the controller first.
  4. On a new Koala installation, Koala Link opens Koala once so it can create its settings. Complete any welcome or permission dialogs, then quit Koala when asked. Koala Link then prepares the MIDI mappings and reopens Koala. You do not need to do any MIDI Learn.
  5. For MPC Touch, allow Koala Link and mpc_touchd in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility when asked.
Upgrading from an earlier version keeps your settings. Your Koala settings and MIDI maps are backed up before any change.

4. Push 2 (preview)

Push 2 support is a preview: the pads, buttons, encoders and screen are mapped, but full hardware testing is still going on. The chop-grid fix in this version was confirmed on physical Push hardware. The complete control list is in PUSH2-CONTROLS.md on the project's GitHub page.

Choose a sample and play its chops

  1. Create or edit your sample's chops in Koala.
  2. Press NOTE or the upper KEYBOARD button on Push. The grid blinks while waiting for a source.
  3. Hit the loaded sample pad you want to use. Empty pads leave the chooser waiting.
  4. Play its chops across all 64 pads. The chop grid is cyan; held pads turn white. Other loaded samples cannot take over the grid.
  5. Press Keyboard again to exit. Re-enter Keyboard to choose a different sample.
Push session ownership: on first use, approve the prompt to use Push exclusively with Koala Link. Its raw MIDI ports are hidden from other apps while the session runs, preventing duplicate pad input in Koala. Close Ableton Live and other Push apps first. Quitting Koala Link or changing controllers restores the ports. After a crash, reopen Koala Link and choose Push to recover them.

5. Webcam

Webcam films you playing, with Koala's own sound, the pad you hit and its waveform, and video effects that follow Koala's Perform effects. Open it from the start screen or the Koala Link menu (Webcam…).

Video effects

  • STUTTER repeats the picture in time with Koala's tempo. A small turn of Q-Link D1 repeats every quarter note, and turning further goes to 8ths, 16ths and 32nds. Each repeat fades in smoothly. Turn it all the way down and the video plays normally again.
  • CUTTER cuts the picture to black in slices at the same kind of rate, set by Q-Link D4.
  • HOLD FOR STUTTER stutters the video while you hold the button. LEARN KOALA'S STUTTER lets the video follow Koala's own on-screen STUTTER after you touch it once.

Sound out

ControlWhat it does
MeterThe recording's level in dB.
DRIVE0 to 12 dB of gain into the recording.
MPC3000 OUTAn MPC3000-style clipper for a punchier sound.
LIMITERKeeps peaks under -1 dB. Its threshold goes from 0 to -20 dB.

Recording

  • RECORD starts and stops a recording.
  • ARM (on the floating preview) waits for your first pad hit and starts recording then.
  • FLOATING PREVIEW is a small window that stays on top of Koala while you make the beat.
  • OUTPUT WINDOW (FOR OBS) is a clean picture to capture in OBS for live streaming.
  • Files are MP4: H.264 video at 30 fps (about 11 Mbps) and AAC stereo sound at 48 kHz, 256 kbps. They are ready to upload to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube as they are.

6. Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
The app will not openSystem Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
The MPC screen is blankInstall DisplayLink Manager and reconnect the MPC Touch.
Touches do nothingAllow Koala Link and mpc_touchd under Accessibility, then restart Koala Link.
Pads or controls do nothing in KoalaQuit Koala and choose your controller again in Koala Link so it can repair the mapping.
No sound in a Webcam recordingAllow Koala Link under Screen & System Audio Recording, and check that Sound is set to Koala.
No camera pictureAllow Koala Link under Camera.
