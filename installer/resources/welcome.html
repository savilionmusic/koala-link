Koala Link

Koala Sampler on the Akai MPC Touch: Koala fills the MPC's screen, and the MPC's 64 pads (banks A–D), Q-Links, 16 LEVELS, REC and PLAY play it.

This installs:

  • Koala Link, in your Applications folder;
  • the MPC Touch touch-screen driver and its tools (the same driver Paper Cut uses): set up now if an MPC Touch is plugged in, otherwise Koala Link sets it up the first time it runs with one.

Koala Sampler itself comes from the Mac App Store (elf audio); it isn't part of this installer.
