Koala Link

Koala Sampler with an Akai MPC Touch or Ableton Push 2. Choose your controller in Koala Link. Push 2 is a preview; physical Push hardware validation is pending.

This installs:

  • Koala Link, in your Applications folder;
  • the shared MPC Touch touch-screen driver and its tools (also used by Paper Cut). It is used only when you choose MPC Touch; Push 2 does not require it.

Koala Sampler itself comes from the Mac App Store (elf audio); it isn't part of this installer.

For Push 2, connect and power it by USB before choosing it, and close Ableton Live and Paper Cut. Koala opens in its ordinary Mac window while Koala Link provides the Push display.
