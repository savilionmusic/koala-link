Koala Link is installed

  1. Open Koala Link from Applications with the MPC Touch connected.
  2. The first time, macOS asks to allow Koala Link in Privacy & Security › Accessibility (it places Koala's window on the MPC's screen). If System Settings opened there, also switch mpc_touchd on: that's the MPC's touch screen.
  3. Koala opens full screen on the MPC. Play.

To remove it: /Library/Application Support/Koala Touch/uninstall.sh in Terminal.
