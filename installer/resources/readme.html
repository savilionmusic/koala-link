How it works

  • Open Koala Link and choose MPC Touch or Push 2. MPC Touch readies its touch service, sets Koala's MIDI map, and opens Koala full screen on the MPC. Push 2 uses its own display and opens Koala in its ordinary Mac window.
  • The MPC's pads are Koala's pads: bank buttons A–D pick the bank, and the on-screen bank strip follows. The Q-Links set start, length, volume and pitch; REC and PLAY control Koala.
  • FULL LEVEL toggles maximum pad velocity: light on sends 127 for every hit; light off preserves how hard you play. It works in all four banks, for samples and Chopper notes. MIDI and keyboard velocity are enabled in Koala for this control.
  • 16 LEVELS toggles Chopper keyboard mode: bank A pad 1 plays chop 1, starting at MIDI 60 (Koala's C3). Banks A–D play chops 1–64 using notes 60–75, 76–91, 92–107 and 108–123. With fewer chops, Koala wraps across the available slices; with 128 chops, these banks play the first 64. Chopper mode starts at bank A; press it again to restore your sample bank. Koala Link also follows the restored song's keyboard mode and the on-screen Sequence keyboard button.
  • Quit Koala, and Koala Link lets the MPC go and quits too.
  • Push 2 preview: its 64 pads play sample banks, 16 Levels or Chopper slices; encoders and the touch strip control the displayed Koala pages. Push 2 has not yet been validated on physical hardware.

Requirements

  • An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later, and Koala Sampler for Mac.
  • Either an Akai MPC Touch by USB with DisplayLink Manager (from Synaptics, displaylink.com) for its screen, or an Ableton Push 2 connected and powered by USB.
  • DisplayLink Manager and the MPC touch driver's Accessibility permission are needed only for MPC Touch. Close Ableton Live while Koala Link uses the Push display, and quit Paper Cut if it is using the Push.
  • Koala Link and Paper Cut share the MPC touch service; run one controller app at a time when using MPC Touch.
