How it works

  • Open Koala Touch. It readies the MPC Touch, sets Koala's MIDI map, and opens Koala full screen on the MPC's screen.
  • The MPC's pads are Koala's pads: bank buttons A–D pick the bank, and the on-screen bank strip follows. The Q-Links set start, length, volume and pitch; REC and PLAY control Koala.
  • 16 LEVELS toggles Chopper keyboard mode: banks A–D send 64 chromatic notes starting at C−1 (MIDI 0). A sends 0–15, B 16–31, C 32–47 and D 48–63. Press it again for the normal sample pads. Use the physical button to keep the mapping and Koala's keyboard mode together.
  • Quit Koala, and Koala Touch lets the MPC go and quits too.

Requirements

  • An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later, and Koala Sampler for Mac.
  • An Akai MPC Touch by USB, and DisplayLink Manager (from Synaptics, displaylink.com) for its screen.
  • Koala Touch and Paper Cut both use the MPC Touch, so run one at a time.
